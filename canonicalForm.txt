Canonical Specification: Expression (Addition)1. Node DefinitionAn Expression represents a sum of terms. It consists of two internal collections:constants_list: A list of pure constant terms (no variables).nonconstants_list: A list of Term nodes containing at least one variable.2. Structural ConstraintsFlattening: An Expression node cannot be a direct child of another Expression. Nested sums must be merged into the parent lists.Degenerate Cases:If the result contains 0 terms $\rightarrow$ Return Fraction(0).If the result contains 1 term $\rightarrow$ Return that term (e.g., a single Fraction, Symbol, or Term).Coefficient Storage: Nonconstant terms store their constant part as a single "Constant Expression" (the coefficient) and their variable part as a product (the base).3. Distribution & Expansion RulesThe system follows a Modified Sum-of-Products (SOP) model to ensure variable isolation:Constant Over Expression: If a constant $C$ is multiplied by an Expression $(A + B)$, it must be distributed: $CA + CB$.Example: $\pi(x + 1) \rightarrow \pi x + \pi$.Nonconstant Coefficient: A constant expression acting as a coefficient to a variable base does not distribute into the variable base.Example: $(\pi + 1)x$ is canonical; it is not expanded to $\pi x + x$.Pure Constant Distribution: Within the constants_list, all products must be fully distributed over sums to avoid symbolic GCD requirements.Example: $(2 + \sqrt{2})\pi \rightarrow 2\pi + \sqrt{2}\pi$.4. Combining Like TermsTo ensure uniqueness, the Expression performs the following grouping logic:Nonconstant Part:Identify Base: The product of all nonconstant factors (e.g., $x \cdot y$).Sum Coefficients: Collect all constant coefficients for that base.Simplify Coefficient: Run the constant sum through a recursive constant simplifier.Result: If simplified coefficient is $0$, drop the term. Else, create Term([simplified_coeff], [base]).Constant Part:Identify Base: The product of all non-rational primitive constants (e.g., $\pi \cdot \sqrt{5}$).Sum Rationals: Group by the rational Fraction part.Result: Rebuild as simplified distributed constant terms.5. Sorting & OrderingNodes are sorted within their respective lists using a multi-step getSortOrder() tuple:Type Priority: Fraction < Constant Symbol < Power < Function < Term < Expression.Lexicographic/Value Tie-break: * Fractions: Sorted by numeric value.Symbols/Functions: Sorted alphabetically by name.Composite Nodes: Sorted by the sort-order of their children recursively.6. Canonical Philosophy summaryFeatureRuleVariablesGrouped by coefficient to facilitate solving ($Ax + B$ form).ConstantsFully distributed to facilitate simplification ($2\pi + 3\pi \rightarrow 5\pi$).IntegrityAll nodes are simplified recursively before being added to an Expression.
